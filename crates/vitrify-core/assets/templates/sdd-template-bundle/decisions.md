# Decisions & Open Questions

Append-only register. `D-###` — decisions this SDD makes itself (dated, with
a one-line rationale). `Q-###` — open questions discovered while building;
each names and blocks the units it affects until resolved. A question is
open unless its entry says `resolved`: `- **Q-001** ([YYYY-MM-DD], open) — …`
becomes `([YYYY-MM-DD], resolved [YYYY-MM-DD])`.

- **D-001** ([YYYY-MM-DD], SDD 0.1.0) — [decision + rationale].
